# Project overview
## Purpose
A multi-user content planning assistant known as CadencePilot - that turns brand goals, available time and evidence-backed ideas into an achievable weekly creation schedule. It helps creators understand topics, prepare material and track actual creation/publication without spending their week maintaining a planner.

## Users and boundaries
Platform owner Ahmad approves access. Each approved customer has a private workspace with multiple brands; workspace membership exists in the model, but team invitation UI is deferred. Owner operations do not automatically expose customer conversations. Optional future support access must be explicit and audited.
Ahmad's initial brand names are Codex Foundry, Narrative Doc and Centera Index. Their subjects, audiences and tones are unknown and must be collected rather than fabricated. Names are user data, never source-code special cases. Availability can include Sunday, Tuesday, Wednesday and Thursday evenings, subject to work shifts; exact durations and timezone are onboarding inputs.

## Essential journey
Public demonstration -> authenticated access request -> owner approval -> user/brand onboarding -> goals and realistic capacity -> proposed weekly plan -> essential topic research -> user accepts plan -> click topic for research and drafts -> record/edit/publish across chosen platforms -> tick actual outcomes -> weekly review and next plan.
AI is inside the application: questionnaire, brand chat, contextual retrieval, topic proposals, research, planning, drafting and reflective suggestions. Manual prompt export/import is a later fallback, not the main experience.

## First release
Premium public website, owner approvals, workspace isolation, configurable multiple brands/platforms, adaptive questionnaire, editable memories, weekly plans, linked originals/derivatives/publications, research briefs with sources, content outlines/captions, availability-aware tasks, reminders and basic progress. Topic titles open briefs; checkboxes have separate completion actions.

## Deferred
Automatic social publishing, platform analytics imports, media editing/hosting at scale, automatic clip cutting, billing, team invitations, marketing analytics and autonomous external actions. Store links/transcripts and lightweight metadata initially. Timestamped clip ranges require a timestamped transcript; otherwise offer conceptual shorts.

## Product rules
Weekly target is an editable preference, not a promise of available capacity. Permanent target changes and this-week overrides are separate. Plans are proposals until accepted. Research can run while a topic appears as Researching, but cannot be labelled Ready to create until the essential brief passes its checks. User can record real work even if research is incomplete; that action does not validate research quality.
Recorded, edited and published are different facts. One original posted on three platforms is one original and three publications. Suggestions never count as completed work. Reopening an item corrects statistics. External results such as views are unavailable unless entered/imported; do not infer brand growth from completed checkboxes.
